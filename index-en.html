The PM-Multiscale Ontology defines terminology for permanent magnet multi-scale simulation, covering electronic, atomistic, mesoscopic, and continuum models and their bridging parameters. It extends EMMO and MagMO with domain-specific classes for simulation workflows, material properties, and data provenance.
